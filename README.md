# Rhythm Sight-Reader — Free Online Bass & Rhythm Sight-Reading Trainer

A zero-install, browser-based rhythm sight-reading trainer designed for bassists and rhythm musicians.

- **Live App (GitHub Pages)**: https://gnosia1104.github.io/rhythm-practice/

## Features
- **Print-Quality Vector SVG Bass-Clef Rhythm Generator**: 6 progressive levels (Quarter Basics → Straight 8ths → 8th Off-beats → Syncopation → 16th Basics → 16th Notes Advanced), 5 time signatures (`4/4`, `3/4`, `2/4`, `5/4`, `6/8`), 2–32 bars, PDF print-ready
- **Bass Articulation & Rhythm Toggles**: Independent Triplets (8th & 2-beat), Staccato (`•` 50% reverse-sustain grading), and Ghost Notes (`×` percussive dead-note grading)
- **3-Way Input Toggles**: Keyboard (`Space`/`J`/`K`, `F`/`D` ghost), Mobile Two-Finger Touch Pads, and real **Audio Interface / Mic** input (multi-channel support, 35–1200 Hz bass bandpass detector, auto noise-floor calibration, 16-beat tap latency calibration)
- **Precision Millisecond Grading**: `±35ms` tight pocket, timing distribution scatter plot, rest violation detection, audio sustain & mute tracking, Live / Reveal-at-End (Blind) grading, and `S+`–`C` rank tiers
- **Daily Challenges, Custom Editor & Score Brag Cards**: Worldwide `Daily` (4/4) & `Daily+` (odd meters) challenges with streaks, serverless Custom Rhythm Score Editor (`?custom=...`), self-contained all-in-one seed URLs (`?seed=54-L4T2SG-8B-85-A1B2`), and downloadable 1200×630 PNG Score Brag Cards
- **Special Training Drills (Beta)**: Gap Click metronome (`1+1`, `2+2`, `1+3`), Look-ahead Fade-out (`1b`/`2b`), and BPM Rush sudden death (up to 180 BPM)
- **Multilingual UI & Interactive Onboarding**: Korean (`한국어`), English, Japanese (`日本語`) with first-visit interactive spotlight tour and IME-independent shortcuts
